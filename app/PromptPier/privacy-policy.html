Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how PromptPier collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Chrome extension and related cloud sync services.

1. Who We Are

PromptPier is a browser extension and related service that helps you save, organize, sync, and insert AI prompts into supported websites such as ChatGPT and DeepSeek.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use PromptPier, we may process the following information:

  • Google account information — when you sign in with Google, we receive basic profile data permitted by your authorization (such as email address, display name, and profile picture) to identify your account.
  • Prompt content you create — titles, body text, tags, color markers, favorites, and timestamps for prompts you save in PromptPier.
  • Sync metadata — revision counters and change records needed to keep your prompts consistent across devices.
  • Local extension data — session tokens, cached prompt lists, adapter configuration, and language preferences stored in your browser.

We do not require a separate password. We do not read your full chat history on third-party AI websites. The extension only interacts with input fields when you explicitly insert a prompt or use the in-page picker.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information above to:

  • authenticate you and associate data with your account;
  • store and sync your prompts across the extension and web client;
  • insert selected prompt text into supported pages at your request;
  • provide search, filtering, and organization features;
  • maintain service security and troubleshoot errors.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Where Data Is Stored

  • Cloud storage — account metadata and synced prompts are stored on Cloudflare Workers KV infrastructure operated for PromptPier.
  • Your browser — the extension stores session and cache data locally using Chrome extension storage APIs.

When you are offline, the extension may continue to show locally cached prompts. Changes are synced when connectivity is restored.

5. Permissions and On-Page Behavior

The Chrome extension may request permissions such as:

  • identity — Google sign-in;
  • storage — save prompts and settings locally;
  • activeTab — interact with the current tab when you insert a prompt;
  • clipboardWrite — copy prompt text when a page cannot be injected;
  • sidePanel — show the PromptPier management panel;
  • host access — connect to the PromptPier API and run content scripts on web pages you visit.

Content scripts are used only to detect supported chat inputs, show the optional ~ prompt picker, and insert text you choose. They are not used to collect unrelated page content.

6. Third-Party Services

PromptPier relies on third-party services including:

  • Google — OAuth sign-in and account verification;
  • Cloudflare — API hosting and prompt storage.

Your use of Google sign-in is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account data and prompts while your account remains active and as needed to provide the service. Deleted prompts are handled using soft-delete semantics in sync data and may remain in backups for a limited period consistent with operational practices.

To request account or data deletion, contact bolidezjb@gmail.com. You can also remove local extension data by uninstalling the extension and clearing browser extension storage.

8. Security

We use HTTPS for API communication and issue authenticated access tokens after Google sign-in. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

9. Children's Privacy

PromptPier is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the service, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the latest revision. Continued use of PromptPier after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email bolidezjb@gmail.com.
